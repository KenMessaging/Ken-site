Your people. Your space.

Everything that makes Ken.

This list brings together the app and our feature vision. Availability varies by release; it isn't a list of features you can use today.

Conversations

  • End-to-end encrypted one-to-one and group chats
  • Photos, videos, voice messages and files
  • Voice and video calling
  • Reactions, replies and pinned messages
  • Scheduled and disappearing messages
  • Chat backgrounds and bubble colours

Communities

  • Public and private communities
  • Eight-digit codes and QR joining
  • Photo posts, banners and discussions
  • Events, locations and private RSVPs
  • Engagement and like sorting
  • Private applications and admin approvals

Your pace

  • Online, Busy and custom statuses
  • Relax mode: hold incoming chats and silence calls
  • App Lock and biometric protection
  • Wi-Fi-only networking and link previews
  • Blocking, reporting and local spam filtering
  • Privacy-conscious notification controls

Find your people

  • Username-based accounts
  • Contact QR scanning and photo import
  • Phone contact matching
  • Opt-in nearby discovery
  • Offline Wi-Fi/Bluetooth messaging
  • Optional Tor routing

Memories and Ken+

  • Encrypted photo and video stories
  • 30 GB encrypted media storage
  • Ken Cloud and device recovery
  • Custom app icons and personalization
  • Optional premium membership
