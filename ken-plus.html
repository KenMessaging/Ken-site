Ken+ is our planned way to fund Ken: optional encrypted storage that helps cover the infrastructure and care a private messenger needs. Thank you for wanting to be part of it.

Not a checkout. This page previews the planned offering. A recipient username entered below stays in this page's memory; it is not submitted or saved by this site. No payment provider is connected.

01. Choose your plan

Both plans include the same proposed features. Prices shown in EUR; final tax and renewal disclosures will be confirmed before sales begin. Crypto uses a prepaid duration instead.

Choose how many months

1–24 months. Each full 12-month block costs €48; additional months cost €8 each. For the lowest price over a year, choose a full 12-month block: 11 months cost €88, while 12 months cost €48. A one-time payment, with no automatic renewal. These are EUR preview prices, not cryptocurrency quotes.

02. What's planned

Your encrypted media album.

30 GB for supported photos and videos, encrypted on your device before upload. A simple four-column gallery to keep your memories together.

Ken Cloud.

Planned encrypted backups for supported messages, contacts and settings, for a phone move or an extra copy. Recovery and device transfer are not available yet. Ken Cloud quota details will be published before purchase.

For you. Or someone you care about.

Use your own username or the person you want to give Ken+ to. Preview only: no account lookup or activation takes place. Never enter a password, PIN or recovery key.

The planned benefit belongs to the recipient account and cannot be transferred after activation. A future purchase must link the entitlement to that account; a username does not make payment anonymous. Providers and public blockchains may retain transaction records.

If you stop subscribing.

The proposed policy is to remove unpaid stored media after two years without renewal. It is not an implemented retention guarantee. Exact deadlines, notice, export, backup deletion and reactivation rules must be finalized before subscriptions open.

Cancellation, refunds, withdrawal rights, recurring-payment consent and applicable regional rules will be stated before you can buy. No blanket waiver of statutory consumer rights applies to this preview.
