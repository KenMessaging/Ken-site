Privacy overview

The website

This preview has no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts or payment scripts. Recipient usernames, optional donation names and donation amounts stay in page memory and are not submitted or saved by this site. Only selecting cryptocurrency on the Ken+ preview contacts CoinGecko for a EUR market rate; CoinGecko can process the visitor's IP address and connection information, but the request contains no recipient username. Estimates expire after eight minutes and are not guaranteed payment quotes. The donation page does not contact an exchange-rate service. GitHub Pages or another hosting provider can process IP addresses, request headers and connection logs to serve the site. Email links open your chosen email service; the correspondence is handled by that service and Proton Mail, not Ken's end-to-end encryption.

The app

Ken processes usernames, password-verification data, account/session information, public encryption keys, encrypted payloads, delivery metadata and enabled account settings. Optional verified contact details are processed only when those features are used. Support/report metadata, staff handling and backups have separate retention and access requirements. We do not claim “no data collection.”

Supported private messages and attachments use end-to-end encryption. Public community names, descriptions and membership identities are visible to signed-in users. Your identity is not anonymous simply because content is encrypted. Recipients can copy messages; screenshots and independently saved files cannot be recalled.

Optional Tor routing is not device-wide anonymity. Apple push, Maps, email and external payment/browser apps are outside Ken's Tor route. A production push service sends Apple a generic alert and an opaque short-lived reference, not message text; Apple still processes device tokens and connection metadata.

Recovery, retention and deletion

Account recovery does not restore lost device keys or history. Account deletion in the app requires current-password confirmation and network access. Other users' copies and shared signed community history may remain. Backup expiry, deletion-journal reconciliation, applicable legal holds and provider retention must be accurately documented before launch; no instant deletion of every copy is promised.

Final controller identity, legal bases, provider locations, international transfers, exact retention, supervisory-authority details and applicable rights notices remain pending. Send privacy requests to Kenmessaging@proton.me. Include only necessary account context. Do not send passwords, PINs, keys or identity documents. Responses and any justified extensions must comply with applicable deadlines; email is not an emergency service.

Status and optional app usage

The status and Relax mode update keeps status off by default. When enabled, your status text is visible to unblocked contacts with an existing direct chat; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Online can reflect Apple's shared notifications-silenced state while Ken is open, but Ken cannot identify which Focus mode is active. Relax mode holds direct and group deliveries within existing retention limits; ordinary communities keep syncing. Distinct callers are temporarily recorded for your resume summary, then those caller records are removed. Resume counts remain until your next pause or account deletion.

Optional app usage reporting is off by default and separate from message delivery. Where available, you can opt in under Privacy to share approximate foreground time by feature and counts of settings changes. Samples contain a random submission identifier, feature category, bounded time or change count and consent, never message content, contacts or setting values. Requests use your signed-in connection; usage storage keeps daily totals and aggregate audience sketches rather than account or device lists. Only the configured owner can view these aggregates. This is not a per-person activity dashboard.

Aggregate usage data has a 370-day retention window and submission-deduplication receipts a two-day window. Cleanup runs on submission, at server startup and every six hours; encrypted backups follow their separate retention policy. Opting out stops future submissions, but already combined aggregate totals cannot be separated for deletion. Time and active-user estimates are approximate, not billing records. Visiting this website does not activate app usage collection.

Solana-network donations

The Support Ken page provides one wallet address and a locally hosted QR code for optional, one-time SOL, USDC and EURC donations on the Solana network. No Ken username or login is requested. Euro amount choices are a guide only, not payment quotes. Copy buttons write the public donation address or your chosen memo to your clipboard; the website does not initiate or submit a transfer. An optional donation name stays in page memory unless you copy it into your wallet's transaction memo and send it. Published names, addresses and amounts are public, cannot be erased from the blockchain and are not verified identities.

The activity lists load from a separate Cloudflare-hosted service, which processes IP addresses and connection metadata when serving requests. That service stores public transaction signatures, sending addresses, supported amounts, timestamps and public donation memos in Cloudflare D1. It reads finalized history through Helius and historical market prices through CoinGecko; your typed name and donation amount guide are not sent to these services. Latest donations and cumulative per-address rankings cover supported direct transfers only. EUR values are historical hourly estimates, not current prices or guaranteed conversion values. Incomplete history or missing prices withhold the all-time ranking.

Your wallet provider handles the transfer. Wallet addresses, amounts and transactions are public on the blockchain and cannot be erased by Ken. Using a wallet does not guarantee anonymity. Check the destination, currency and Solana network before sending; transfers are generally irreversible and network fees apply. Donations are not automatically converted to fiat, do not activate Ken+ and carry no promise of future features or tax-deductibility. This page provides a Solana wallet address for direct transfers.

Terms overview

Ken is not a guaranteed or emergency communications service. Keep your credentials and device keys safe. Do not impersonate others or use the service for unlawful exploitation, abuse, threats, harassment, fraud, spam, malware or interference.

You retain your content rights and must have permission to share content. Only the permissions necessary to provide the service are intended; recipients may retain their own copies. Reports do not automatically prove wrongdoing. Restrictions and appeals require a staffed, lawful process.

Ken+ and billing

The Ken+ page describes proposed pricing of €8/month or €48/year, 30 GB media storage and Ken Cloud. This website does not process subscriptions or payments. Taxes, automatic renewal, cancellation, withdrawal rights, refunds and region-specific purchase rules must be confirmed before any purchase. No statutory consumer rights are waived here.

Final operator details, contractual eligibility, complaints procedure and jurisdiction-specific terms require review before launch. Draft text is not presented for acceptance.

Safety & support

The operator has designated Kenmessaging@proton.me for support, privacy and abuse intake. Staff coverage, response commitments and production handling still require verification. This is not a 24-hour emergency channel.

  • Immediate danger: contact local emergency services. In Poland and Norway, call 112 for emergencies where applicable. Do not wait for an email response.
  • Unwanted contact: use Block and Report spam in Ken. Reporting does not automatically transmit encrypted chat history or notify the reported person.
  • Abuse: send necessary usernames or community identifiers, approximate dates/time zone and non-sensitive context. Share only information you are entitled to provide.
  • Child safety: never attach, forward, download or request suspected child sexual abuse material. Give non-graphic identifiers only. Contact police or an appropriate child-protection reporting service for your location.
  • Appeals: include a case reference, decision date and brief explanation. Do not resend sensitive evidence. Your rights to contact authorities are unaffected.
  • Encrypted tickets: in-app support requires an initialized inbox and independently verified support fingerprint. These keys and staff access must be operationally validated before launch. Email remains a separate channel outside Ken's end-to-end encryption.

Children & eligibility

No “all ages, no conditions” eligibility is asserted. Children's data-protection consent, contractual capacity, parental authorization and store age ratings are distinct issues and vary by jurisdiction. Poland, Norway and any additional launch markets need specific review. The signup puzzle is not age verification. Until arrangements are finalized, these drafts do not authorize a children's launch.
