A few things
worth knowing.

Is Ken free?

Yes. Ken's messaging is free. Ken+ is an optional premium upgrade, not a requirement to chat.

How can I learn more about Ken?

Explore the features and follow product updates on Ken News.

How does Ken protect my privacy?

Your conversations are yours. End-to-end encryption keeps supported private messages and attachments unreadable to our servers. For a clear explanation of account information, message delivery and optional services, read our privacy overview.

Will Ken have voice and video calls?

Yes. Voice and video calling are part of Ken. Our feature list covers the experience we are building.

Can Ken recover my messages if I lose my phone?

Account recovery does not recover lost device encryption keys or chat history. Keep your existing device and keys safe.
